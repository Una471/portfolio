Project overview

Match hotel pricing and service to real demand

A hotel-performance report and pricing tool connecting occupancy, events, revenue and guest feedback.

View the reportUse the solution

The problem

Why this project was needed

The hotel charged similar rates across quiet and high-demand periods. Rooms could sell too cheaply during events, while guest complaints from different platforms were easy to miss.

What the report shows

The evidence in plain language

The report compares occupancy, revenue, booking patterns, event demand and guest feedback across the hotel operation.

Main findings

  • Events and weekends create stronger room demand.
  • Flat pricing leaves revenue on the table during busy periods.
  • Scattered reviews delay responses to guest concerns.

Recommended action

  • Raise rates when occupancy and local demand are high.
  • Use targeted offers during quieter periods.
  • Bring guest reviews into one follow-up process.

The practical response

The hotel tool recommends a room rate from current demand and keeps guest feedback available for timely service recovery.

  • Demand-based pricing
  • Occupancy context
  • Guest follow-up
