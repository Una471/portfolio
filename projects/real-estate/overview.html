Use portfolio evidence to make more consistent property decisions

View the reportUse the solution

The problem

Why this project was needed

Listings, selling time, commission and pricing information were difficult to review together. Valuations and portfolio decisions could depend too heavily on individual judgement.

What the report shows

The evidence in plain language

The report compares portfolio activity, commission results, agents, property types, locations and lead sources.

Main findings

  • Long-running listings may need pricing or marketing review.
  • Missed commission shows where opportunities are being lost.
  • Performance changes by location and property type.

Recommended action

  • Review listings exceeding normal selling time.
  • Use completed deals when setting price expectations.
  • Apply consistent information to every valuation.

The practical response

ValuTrack guides an analyst through property details and produces an estimate supported by comparable portfolio evidence.

  • Structured valuation
  • Comparable evidence
  • Explainable estimate
