Project overview

Use sales evidence to improve stock and promotion decisions

A retail-performance report and inventory tool for understanding customer baskets, promotions and stock needs.

View the reportUse the solution

The problem

Why this project was needed

Products were placed and reordered from experience rather than evidence. Popular items sold out, while promotions continued without a clear view of whether they increased sales.

What the report shows

The evidence in plain language

The report explains sales trends, products commonly bought together, promotion results and stock requiring attention.

Main findings

  • Several frequently paired products were placed far apart.
  • Promotion performance varies significantly by event.
  • Popular items require earlier reorder warnings.

Recommended action

  • Place commonly purchased products closer together.
  • Direct promotion spending toward events that lift sales.
  • Reorder fast-moving products before they reach low stock.

The practical response

The inventory tool turns sales and stock information into straightforward reorder and merchandising actions.

  • Basket patterns
  • Promotion review
  • Reorder alerts
