Project overview

See service delays early and act before targets are missed

CivicTrack connects public-service reporting with a request-screening desk for frontline teams.

View the reportUse the solution

The problem

Why this project was needed

Managers lacked one view of requests across departments and submission channels. Pending cases and approaching deadlines could be difficult to identify before escalation became necessary.

What the report shows

The evidence in plain language

The report compares request volume, completion, on-time service, escalations and completion time.

Main findings

  • Service performance differs between departments.
  • Completion time varies by submission channel.
  • Pending cases are easier to resolve when identified early.

Recommended action

  • Prioritise requests approaching their deadlines.
  • Review bottlenecks in departments with repeated delays.
  • Guide citizens toward reliable channels for each service.

The practical response

The CivicTrack Request Desk reviews an individual case and gives staff a clear handling or escalation recommendation.

  • Request screening
  • Deadline awareness
  • Escalation guidance
