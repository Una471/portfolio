Project overview

Plan maintenance before equipment failure stops production

An equipment-health report and maintenance tool for teams responsible for heavy machinery.

View the reportUse the solution

The problem

Why this project was needed

Unexpected breakdowns caused expensive emergency repairs and production delays, even though temperature, vibration, oil and pressure readings were already being collected.

What the report shows

The evidence in plain language

The report shows fleet condition, failure patterns, maintenance cost and the machines presenting the strongest warning signs.

Main findings

  • Temperature and vibration rise before many failures.
  • Low oil pressure and poor oil condition increase risk.
  • A small group of machines account for much of the downtime.

Recommended action

  • Prioritise machines showing several warning signs.
  • Schedule maintenance before readings reach critical levels.
  • Review repeated idle time and operator-related patterns.

The practical response

The maintenance tool turns current equipment readings into a clear condition result and recommended maintenance priority.

  • Equipment checks
  • Failure warning
  • Maintenance priority
