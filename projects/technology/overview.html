View the reportUse the solution

The problem

Why this project was needed

Customer activity, payments, product use and support history sat in separate records. Teams often responded only after a customer had already decided to leave.

What the report shows

The evidence in plain language

The report compares customer activity, recurring revenue, product use and leaving rates across customer segments.

Main findings

  • Low product use and fewer sign-ins are early warning signs.
  • Smaller and newer accounts require different support.
  • Revenue is clearer when viewed with service and usage information.

Recommended action

  • Prioritise accounts showing several warning signs.
  • Prepare follow-up using each customer's actual circumstances.
  • Review account health again after intervention.

The practical response

RetainIQ combines familiar customer information into a clear risk assessment and practical follow-up plan.

  • Clear risk result
  • Focused follow-up plan
