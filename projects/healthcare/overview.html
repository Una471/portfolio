Project overview

Improve patient flow, medicine use and bed availability

A hospital-operations report and working tool that help staff respond to daily service pressures.

View the reportUse the solution

The problem

Why this project was needed

Patients waited too long, medicines expired before use and emergency staff spent valuable time calling wards to locate available beds.

What the report shows

The evidence in plain language

The report brings waiting time, patient flow, medicine expiry and bed availability into one operational view.

Main findings

  • Afternoon shifts experience longer patient waits.
  • Some medicines approach expiry while remaining overstocked.
  • Bed availability is difficult to coordinate without a shared view.

Recommended action

  • Match staffing levels to the busiest periods.
  • Move near-expiry stock to departments with higher use.
  • Keep ward capacity visible to emergency teams.

The practical response

The hospital tool helps staff review patient flow, pharmacy alerts and available beds without relying on separate manual checks.
