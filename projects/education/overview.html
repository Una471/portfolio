Project overview

Find students who need support before they fall behind

The student report shows where support is needed, while Student Records gives staff one place to manage follow-up.

View the reportUse the solution

The problem

Why this project was needed

Attendance and academic information existed, but staff could not easily compare faculties or recognise learners needing early support. Urgent cases could remain hidden among routine records.

What the report shows

The evidence in plain language

The report compares student numbers, attendance, course results, completion and satisfaction across faculties.

Main findings

  • Lower attendance often appears before weaker results.
  • Completion levels differ between faculties.
  • Advisors need a clear order for student follow-up.

Recommended action

  • Contact students when attendance begins to fall.
  • Target support toward faculties with the widest gaps.
  • Keep intervention notes with each student record.

The practical response

Student Records helps staff register learners, review their circumstances and keep a clear record of support provided.
